# Harbor inventory fixture

Harbor maintains three shelves. Shelf A contains 14 red boxes and 8 blue boxes.
Shelf B contains 9 red boxes and 17 blue boxes. Shelf C contains 11 red boxes and 6 blue boxes.
Every red box weighs 3 kg. Every blue box weighs 2 kg. Each shelf supports 100 kg.

A delivery adds 4 red boxes to shelf A and removes 5 blue boxes from shelf B.
A proposed transfer moves 3 red boxes from shelf C to shelf B. Transfers happen after delivery.
The warehouse opens Monday through Friday, from 09:00 to 17:00. Inspections take 20 minutes
per shelf. Two inspectors may work on different shelves simultaneously.

The report audience wants short factual answers. Do not modify files, use the network,
install tools, delegate work, or invoke skills. Read this fixture when needed.
